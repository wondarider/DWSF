Digital Wellness
Security Framework

Bridging the gap between cybersecurity and child online safety — for parents and educators.

As a parent and cyber security professional, I've seen firsthand how online safety is becoming increasingly complex — yet it remains largely overlooked by traditional security companies, leaving educators and parents without clear guidance on how to protect the children in their care.

Inspired by recent research into classifying online risk to children, I am developing a Digital Wellness Security Framework — a practical, accessible tool designed to bridge that gap.

If you are passtionate about helping protect vulnable communities and would like to get involved - I'd love to hear from you.

This framework is an independent, self-funded initiative and is not affiliated with any organisation.
