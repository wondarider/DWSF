Digital Wellbeing
Security Framework

Online predation and digital self-harm are growing threats to young people. While some schools may be doing activities to address this, they often lack systematic response backed by a structured framework to assess, prioritise and manage online risks.

As the digital world becomes more complex, our responsibility for online safety can seem overwhelming. This challenge remains largely overlooked by traditional security companies, leaving educators and parents without a clear approach on how to protect their children.

Leveraging cybersecurity principles and inspired by research into classifying online risk to children I am developing the Digital Wellbeing Security Framework — a practical, accessible tool designed to bridge that gap and allow our children to experience the internet without fear.

The Digital Wellbeing Security Framework is a structured, self-assessment tool that gives a school the systematic response online safety requires; by providing a starting point to establish an online safety program, a clear picture of their digital wellbeing maturity, pinpoints exactly where uplift efforts are required, and puts the right resources within easy reach.

If you are passionate about helping protect vulnerable communities and would like to get involved — I'd love to hear from you.

This framework is an independent, self-funded initiative and is not affiliated with any organisation.
